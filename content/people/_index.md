## Join the lab {#join}

Knaap is accepting doctoral students through the department's
[Ph.D. program in Urban and Environmental Planning and Policy](https://uppp.soceco.uci.edu/pages/phd-program-admissions).
Students in the program are selected by its admissions committee, which reads
the whole applicant pool and admits a cohort. Admission does not rest on an
individual faculty member's choice or grant funding, so there is no separate
route into the lab and nothing to arrange before you apply.

The way to join is a strong application. Say in your statement of purpose what
you want to study and why the lab's work fits it; the department counts the
statement among the most important parts of the committee's review.

Please do not email Knaap to ask about admission, funding, or whether he is
taking students: the answer is the same for every applicant and is given here,
and the department does not require applicants to contact faculty. Questions
about requirements, deadlines, and fees go to Social Ecology Graduate
Admissions at [seambassador@uci.edu](mailto:seambassador@uci.edu). Deadlines
are posted on the School of Social Ecology's
[application page](https://grads.soceco.uci.edu/pages/application-deadlines-instructions),
and applications go through the
[UCI Graduate Division](https://apply.grad.uci.edu/apply/).
