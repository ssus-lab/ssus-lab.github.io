The project develops methods for neighborhood analysis in education research and
builds analytical capacity among education researchers and practitioners. Much
of that work went into extending [geosnap](https://github.com/oturns/geosnap).

[Grant record](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/about/committed-grants/2020/11/INV024366)
