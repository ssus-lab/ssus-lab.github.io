Drawing on spatial data science, anthropology, and public health, the project
studies the spatial contexts of opioid overdoses in Riverside County,
California, in partnership with local public health agencies and emergency
services. It pairs ethnographic fieldwork with spatial analysis of fatal
overdoses.

Publications:
[Exploring Space-Time Interactions in Fatal Opioid Overdoses](https://doi.org/10.1111/gean.70019)
(*Geographical Analysis*, 2025);
[The emergence of fentanyl in a stimulant landscape](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drugpo.2025.104807)
(*International Journal of Drug Policy*, 2025).
