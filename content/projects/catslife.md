The Colorado Adoption/Twin Study of Lifespan Behavioral Development
(CATSLife) has followed twins, siblings, and families for more
than thirty years. Its current phase adds a focus on sociospatial context: Eli
contributes high-resolution neighborhood data and the methods to examine how
neighborhood influences and individual outcomes change together over time.
