The geography of opportunity names the ways neighborhoods shape outcomes over the
life course. Eli's models of it formed the foundation of HUD's
[formal evaluation](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/portal/sites/default/files/pdf/Thompson-Report.pdf)
of the Baltimore Housing Mobility Program, the remedy in *Thompson v. HUD*, and
the basis of large parts of the Baltimore
[Regional Plan for Sustainable Development](https://www.baltometro.org/community/plans/regional-plan-for-sustainable-development),
developed at the National Center for Smart Growth. The same approach later
informed [Opportunity360](https://dashboards.mysidewalk.com/opportunity360-community-dashboard/welcome)
at Enterprise Community Partners.

Publications:
[The Cartography of Opportunity](https://doi.org/10.1080/10511482.2017.1331930)
(*Housing Policy Debate*, 2017);
[Opportunity for Whom?](https://doi.org/10.1111/cico.12318)
(*City & Community*, 2018).
