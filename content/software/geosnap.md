[geosnap](https://github.com/oturns/geosnap) brings census and other public
data, boundary harmonization, geodemographic clustering and regionalization, and
models of neighborhood change into one spatiotemporal pipeline. Its guide is at
[oturns.github.io/geosnap-guide](https://oturns.github.io/geosnap-guide).
