[segregation](https://github.com/pysal/segregation) implements the segregation
literature's indices with the statistical machinery to compare them: inference
by simulation, decomposition of differences, and spatial versions of the
classic measures. Described in the
[Journal of Open Source Software](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.11126) (2026).
