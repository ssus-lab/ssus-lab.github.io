---
title: "Spatial Science and Urban Systems"
statement: "We build open-source methods, models, and software for understanding sociospatial structure."
---

SSUS is the research lab of [Elijah Knaap](people/eli-knaap/) at the
University of California, Irvine. We study how cities are organized, how
influence crosses boundaries, and how place shapes outcomes.

Our questions come from public decisions. Our work has analyzed a
fair-housing remedy, a regional plan, and the siting of harm-reduction
services, and in each the method mattered because the decision did: a model
that misreads spatial structure sends vouchers, investment, or clinics to the
wrong places. We release the tools as software so others can check the
analysis and use it on their own decisions.
